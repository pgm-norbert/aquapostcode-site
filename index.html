Where the data comes from

Readings come from the Environment Agency water quality archive and the Drinking Water Inspectorate public registers, plus the water companies' own published sampling data. Every figure in the app names its source so you can check it.

Why does my postcode show no readings?

Sampling data is published per zone, not per household, and some water companies publish less than others. Where no sample exists for your area, the app falls back to the typical hardness for your supplier and says so.

Is this a test of my own tap?

No. AquaPostcode reports published testing data for your area. Readings at your own tap can differ, particularly for lead, which usually comes from a property's own pipework. It is not medical or safety advice. If you are concerned about your own supply, contact your water company, who must test on request.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you choose to watch a postcode, your device re-checks the published data itself roughly once a day and tells you if new results appear. UK results are usually updated only every few months, so it is normally quiet. You can turn notifications off in your phone's Settings.

Product recommendations

Where a reading is flagged, the app suggests filters that address that specific reading. These are affiliate links and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Which products appear is decided by your postcode's readings, not by commission.
