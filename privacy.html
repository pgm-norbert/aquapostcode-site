AquaPostcode — Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

AquaPostcode shows publicly published water quality data for a UK postcode. There is no account, no sign-up and no email address. This policy describes exactly what leaves your device.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind.
  • No GPS or device location. The app never requests location permission.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), and no third-party ad or tracking SDKs.
  • We never receive your full postcode. See below.

Your postcode

When you look up a postcode, the full postcode is sent to two public services so the lookup can work:

ServiceWhy
postcodes.ioConverts the postcode to a location and administrative area.
Environment Agency & water company open dataReturns the published sampling results for that area.

These are the same public data sources anyone can query directly. We do not operate a server, and your postcode is never stored by us.

If you turn on watching for a postcode, that postcode is saved on your device only, so the app can re-check it. Turning watching off deletes it.

Analytics

We use PostHog (EU region) to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Analytics events deliberately exclude your full postcode. We send the outcode only, meaning the first part such as SW1A, never SW1A 1AA.

EventWhat it contains
postcode_checkedOutcode, water company, hardness value, which readings were flagged, how many products were shown.
product_clickedWhich product was tapped, its price and position, and the reading it was recommended for.
watch_*Whether you enabled watching and whether a notification was raised.

PostHog also records a random anonymous identifier, your app version, and device model and operating system version. That identifier is not linked to any real-world identity.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and off unless you choose to watch a postcode. They are local notifications: your device periodically re-checks the published data for your saved postcode and raises a notification itself if new results appear. Nothing is pushed from us, we hold no push token, and we do not know which postcodes are being watched.

Affiliate links

Where a water quality reading is flagged, the app may recommend filter products. Those are affiliate links to Waterdrop, and we may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. Which products appear is determined by your postcode's readings, not by commission. Opening a link hands you to Waterdrop's own site, where their privacy policy applies.

Children

AquaPostcode is not directed at children and collects nothing that identifies an individual.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data is held about you, and ask for it to be deleted. Because the app holds no account and stores no personal identifiers, we normally cannot link any record to you. You can clear everything the app has stored by deleting the app.

Accuracy

AquaPostcode reports published testing data for your area. It is not a test of the water at your own tap, and it is not medical or safety advice.
