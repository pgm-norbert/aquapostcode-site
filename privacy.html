AquaPostcode — Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy covers the AquaPostcode app on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). AquaPostcode shows publicly published water quality data for a UK postcode. There is no account, no sign-up and no email address. This policy describes exactly what leaves your device.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind.
  • No GPS or device location. The app never requests location permission.
  • No advertising identifier (Apple IDFA or Android Advertising ID), and no third-party ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No payment card details. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).

Your postcode

When you look up a postcode, the full postcode is sent to two public services so the lookup can work:

ServiceWhy
postcodes.ioConverts the postcode to a location and administrative area.
Environment Agency & water company open dataReturns the published sampling results for that area.

These are the same public data sources anyone can query directly. For ordinary lookups your postcode is not sent to us and is not stored by us.

The Reports tab is different. When you check whether a paid report is available, or buy one, the postcode is sent to the AquaPostcode report service, which we operate (hosted by Render in Frankfurt, Germany). Checking availability does not store the postcode. Buying a report does: see "Paid reports" below.

If you turn on watching for a postcode, that postcode is saved on your device only, so the app can re-check it. Turning watching off deletes it.

Paid reports

A report is an optional one-off In-App Purchase. Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android) takes the payment; we never see your name, Apple ID, Google account, email address or card details. To deliver the report and let you open it again, our report service stores:

  • the postcode the report is for;
  • on iPhone, Apple's transaction identifier and signed receipt data for that purchase, which we check with Apple;
  • on Android, the Google Play order ID for that purchase. The app sends us Google Play's purchase token, which we check with Google Play and use to confirm the purchase with Google; we do not store the token itself;
  • the product bought, the date, and the contents of the report;
  • a one-way hash of the private link token that opens your report.

The app keeps the list of your reports and their private links on your device. Anyone you give a report link to can open that report. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs of requests, which include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting. We keep purchased reports so you can re-open or restore them; to have one deleted, email us with the report ID printed on the report.

Analytics

We use PostHog (EU region) to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Analytics events deliberately exclude your full postcode. We send the outcode only, meaning the first part such as SW1A, never SW1A 1AA.

EventWhat it contains
postcode_checkedOutcode, water company, hardness value, which readings were flagged, how many products were shown.
product_clickedWhich product was tapped, its price and position, and the reading it was recommended for.
report_purchasedWhich report product was bought. No postcode.
watch_*Whether you enabled watching and whether a notification was raised.

PostHog also records a random anonymous identifier, your app version, and device model and operating system version. That identifier is not linked to any real-world identity.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and off unless you choose to watch a postcode. They are local notifications: your device periodically re-checks the published data for your saved postcode and raises a notification itself if new results appear. Nothing is pushed from us, we hold no push token, and we do not know which postcodes are being watched.

Affiliate links

Where a water quality reading is flagged, the app may recommend filter products. Those are links to AquaTru. Where a link is an affiliate link the app says so, and we may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. Which products appear is determined by your postcode's readings, not by commission. Opening a link hands you to AquaTru's own site, where their privacy policy applies.

Children

AquaPostcode is not directed at children and collects nothing that identifies an individual.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data is held about you, and ask for it to be deleted. The app holds no account, so the only record we can link to you is a purchased report, which we can find from its report ID. You can clear everything the app has stored on your device by deleting the app.

Accuracy

AquaPostcode reports published testing data for your area. It is not a test of the water at your own tap, and it is not medical or safety advice.
